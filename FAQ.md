# Project Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What is the core purpose of the system?
The Intelligent Crime Detection and Evidence Management System (ICDEMS) is a dual-layer security platform that combines **Proactive Detection** and **Secure Archiving**. It uses AI to identify criminal activities (such as weapon brandishing or physical violence) in real-time via CCTV feeds and provides a tamper-proof digital locker to store the resulting evidence, ensuring its legal validity for court proceedings.

### 2. How does the "Intelligent" and "Secure" part actually work?
- **Intelligence:** The system utilizes **Computer Vision (YOLOv8)** to analyze live video streams and trigger instant alerts to authorities when a crime is detected.
- **Security:** The system employs **Cryptographic Hashing (SHA-256)** and **Blockchain-inspired logic** to create an immutable "Chain of Custody." This ensures that any attempt to edit or delete evidence is immediately detected and logged.

### 3. Why is this system better than traditional methods?
Traditional security systems are **reactive** (footage is reviewed *after* a crime occurs) and **vulnerable** (digital files can be manipulated). ICDEMS is **proactive** (alerting police *during* the crime) and **transparent** (every interaction with evidence is recorded in a permanent, unchangeable ledger), significantly reducing human error and potential corruption.
